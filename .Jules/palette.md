## 2026-11-05 - Semantic Landmarks & Accessible Sticky Header Skip Navigation
**Learning:** In single-page applications with an 80px (h-20) sticky header, keyboard-only and screen reader users must tab through every navigation item before accessing content unless a dedicated skip link is provided. Without proper scroll padding (`scroll-pt-20`) and target focus management (`tabIndex={-1}` on `<main>`), anchor navigation can visually obscure targeted section headings beneath the sticky bar or fail to shift browser focus correctly.
**Action:** Always provide a high-contrast `Skip to main content` link styled with `focus:fixed focus:z-50 focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-gold` as the very first focusable element, wrap page sections in `<main id="main-content" tabIndex={-1} className="outline-none">`, pair with `motion-safe:scroll-smooth` and `scroll-pt-20`, and ensure sticky navs use a lower z-index (e.g. `z-40`).

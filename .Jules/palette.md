## 2026-10-01 - Semantic Landmarks & Accessible Navigation Controls
**Learning:** Combining a "Skip to Content" link with a `<main>` landmark (`tabIndex={-1}`) and mobile menu ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, dynamic `aria-label`, `aria-controls`) provides a complete accessible foundation for single-page marketing landing pages without visual distraction.
**Action:** Always verify landmark hierarchy and dynamic toggle ARIA attributes alongside CSS scroll-padding (`scroll-pt-20`) when working with sticky header navigation.

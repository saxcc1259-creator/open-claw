## 2026-07-20 - Semantic Foundation
**Learning:** Combining a 'Skip to Content' link targeting a `<main>` landmark with `tabIndex={-1}` and scroll offset utilities (`scroll-pt-20` on the HTML element) ensures beautiful, non-obscured keyboard navigation while shifting focus successfully for screen readers. Dynamic ARIA attributes on mobile menu toggles (`aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`) and marking purely visual icons as `aria-hidden` are critical for reducing visual/auditory noise for screen-reader users.
**Action:** When working on landing pages with sticky headers, always structure them with a skip link, a `<main id="main-content" tabIndex={-1} className="outline-none">` wrapper, proper scroll-padding, and custom, high-contrast, brand-aligned `focus-visible` ring indicators.

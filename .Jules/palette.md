## 2026-06-08 - Semantic Foundation & Scroll Offsets
**Learning:** Combining semantic landmarks (<header>, <main>, <footer>) with a "Skip to Content" link and scroll-padding-top significantly improves the navigation experience for both screen reader and keyboard users in a single-page application with a sticky header. This pattern ensures that focus management and visual alignment are synchronized.
**Action:** Always implement a semantic core (landmarks + skip link + scroll offset) as a first-class citizen in SPA layouts to ensure a robust accessibility foundation.

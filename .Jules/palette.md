## 2026-11-02 - Semantic Accessibility Foundation
**Learning:** A "Skip to Content" link combined with a `<main>` landmark and `scroll-pt-[header-height]` is an essential trio for SPAs with sticky headers. It ensures keyboard users can bypass repetitive navigation and that sections aren't obscured upon focus shift.
**Action:** Always implement this pattern alongside landmark roles and list semantics (`<ul>`/`<li>`) to establish a solid foundation for further UX improvements.

## 2026-06-08 - Semantic Foundation & Sticky Header UX
**Learning:** For single-page apps with sticky headers, combine a 'Skip to Content' link targeting a '<main>' landmark (with tabIndex={-1}) with `scroll-pt-20` and `motion-safe:scroll-smooth` to ensure seamless and non-obscured keyboard navigation.

**Action:** Always implement a semantic landmark foundation early in the project to establish a baseline for accessibility and keyboard navigation.

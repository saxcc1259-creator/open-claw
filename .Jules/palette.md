## 2026-05-07 - Initializing UX/Accessibility Improvements
**Learning:** Found several basic accessibility gaps in the initial landing page template: missing landmarks (main), lack of skip-to-content links, and insufficient ARIA attributes for interactive mobile components. Anchor navigation also obscures headings due to the sticky header.
**Action:** Implementing a "Skip to Content" link, adding a main landmark for better screen reader navigation, and improving mobile menu accessibility with ARIA attributes. Standardizing scroll offsets and smooth scrolling in CSS.

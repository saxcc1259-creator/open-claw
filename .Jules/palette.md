## 2026-06-08 - Brand-Aligned Semantic Foundation
**Learning:** For luxury brand sites, default browser focus rings can feel jarring. Replacing them with a brand-coordinated color (Gold #D4AF37) maintains accessibility without sacrificing the high-end aesthetic. Combining this with a skip-link and proper landmark wrapping creates a "silent" but powerful accessibility layer.
**Action:** Always check if a custom theme color can be used for `focus-visible` rings to make accessibility feel like a first-class citizen of the design rather than an afterthought.

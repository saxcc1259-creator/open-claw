# Palette's Journal - JC Realty & Investment Co.

## 2025-04-25 - Navigation & Accessibility Foundation
**Learning:** Single-page landing pages with sticky headers often suffer from "jump overlap" where the header covers the start of a section upon anchor navigation. Additionally, missing structural landmarks like `<main>` and skip links hinder keyboard-only and screen reader navigation. Customizing focus indicators to match the brand palette (Gold/Navy) ensures accessibility without sacrificing design intent.
**Action:** Always implement `scroll-mt` offsets for sticky headers and ensure proper ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) for interactive mobile menus.
